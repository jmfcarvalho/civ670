GLOBAL PERSPECTIVES

International Innovation Context

Triple & Quadruple Helix

Etzkowitz & Leydesdorff framework integrating University, Industry, and Government. Extended to Quadruple/Quintuple Helices incorporating civil society and environmental sustainability.

Policy Frameworks

OECD Compendium & UNCTAD Framework provide actionable benchmarks. Japanese university venture ecosystems lower barriers for spin-offs and pitch integration.

TTOs & Equity Models

Technology Transfer Offices manage IP and licensing. UK university equity caps (~16%) lower entry barriers for deep-tech academic spin-outs (e.g., Riverlane, SETsquared).

NATIONAL ECOSYSTEM

Brazilian Context & CAPES Metrics

National Innovation System (Law 10,973/04)

  • NITs (Technology Transfer Offices): Mandatory implementation within ICTs to manage IP and licensing (e.g., NIT-UFV).
  • Innovation Habitats: Integration with Science Parks (centev / tecnoPARQ) and incubators.
  • CAPES Technical Output Recognition: Valuation of patents, software registrations, spin-offs, and technological products alongside traditional papers.
LOCAL IMPACT AT UFV

tecnoPARQ & NIT-UFV

Connecting graduate research directly with regional economic growth, spin-off creation, and high-level technical absorption into industry.

Academic Asset Value Technological products generate competitive differentiation for research careers and private ventures.
