PUBLIC SUBVENTION FRAMEWORK

FINEP, FAPEMIG & FNDCT

Economic Subvention represents non-reimbursable public resource allocation directly to corporations for high-risk technological endeavors:

Legal Framework: Innovation Law (Lei 10.973/2004) and Legal Framework for Science, Technology and Innovation (Lei 13.243/2016).
Eligible Expenses: Specialized researchers, prototype fabrication, high-precision third-party testing, certified lab consumables, and international IP filing costs.
Company Matching (Contrapartida): Usually ranges from $5\%$ to $20\%$ for startups/ME/EPP, often allowed as economic matching (dedicated employee salaries).
TRIPARTITE & FISCAL FRAMEWORK

EMBRAPII & Lei do Bem (Law 11.196)

Strategic vehicles for industrial co-development and corporate tax efficiency in mature and scaling engineering ventures:

EMBRAPII Continuous Flux: No calendar deadlines; proposals submitted directly to accredited Units (e.g., SENAI, COPPE, IPT, universities) with decision turnarounds typically in $30$ days.
Financial Model: $33\%$ non-reimbursable funds from EMBRAPII, at least $33\%$ from industrial partners, and up to $34\%$ from the Unit in labor and advanced infrastructure.
Lei do Bem: Applicable to entities under real profit taxation (Lucro Real); provides immediate tax base deductions on technological research expenses.

MODULE 02 — DILUTIVE VENTURE FINANCING

Dilutive Equity, Round Archetypes & Cap Tables

Valuation Mechanics & Equity Dilution

Venture capital exchange involves permanent equity transfer governed by pre-money valuation benchmarks and post-round capital capitalization:

$$V_{\text{post}} = V_{\text{pre}} + I \quad \implies \quad \alpha_{\text{investor}} = \frac{I}{V_{\text{post}}} = \frac{I}{V_{\text{pre}} + I}$$
$V_{\text{pre}}$ (Pre-Money): Agreed enterprise value prior to receiving external cash infusion.
$I$ (Investment): New direct primary capital injected into the corporate treasury.
$V_{\text{post}}$ (Post-Money): Aggregate balance sheet valuation immediately after funding closing.
$\alpha_{\text{investor}}$: Dilution stake purchased by new venture capital partners.
Typical Round Dilution: $15\% - 25\%$

The Option Pool Shuffle Hazard

Creating an ESOP pool (e.g., $10\% - 15\%$) on a pre-money basis effectively dilutes only the founders, reducing their effective pre-money valuation.

VENTURE STAGES TAXONOMY

From Pre-Seed to Series B

Pre-Seed / Angel Round (TRL 3–5) Tickets: $R\$\,200\text{k} - R\$\,1\text{M}$. Team validation, initial lab bench proof-of-concept, customer discovery interviews, and smoke-test traction.
Seed Round (TRL 5–7) Tickets: $R\$\,1.5\text{M} - R\$\,5\text{M}$. Functional field prototype (MVP), early pilot contracts, initial industrial partner letters of intent (LOI).
Series A (TRL 7–8) Tickets: $R\$\,10\text{M} - R\$\,30\text{M}$. Product-Market Fit (PMF) proven, repeatable B2B sales cycle, validated unit economics ($LTV / CAC \ge 3$).
Series B & Scaling (TRL 8–9) Tickets: $R\$\,40\text{M}+$. Full operational scale, geographic expansion, supply chain verticalization, automated manufacturing lines.

MODULE 03 — CONVERTIBLE INSTRUMENTS

SAFE Notes, Convertible Debt & Conversion Mechanics

Conversion Price & Effective Valuation

A SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity) defers enterprise valuation until a priced equity round. The investor converts at the most favorable price between the Valuation Cap and the Discount Rate:

$$P_{\text{conv}} = \min \left( \frac{\text{Valuation Cap}}{N_{\text{shares}}}, \, P_{\text{round}} \times (1 - d) \right)$$
Valuation Cap ($C$): Ceiling valuation that protects early backers if the startup achieves high subsequent valuations.
Discount Rate ($d$): Fixed percentage discount (typically $15\% - 25\%$) against the next priced round share price ($P_{\text{round}}$).
Conversion Shares: Number of shares issued calculated as $S_{\text{safe}} = \frac{I_{\text{safe}}}{P_{\text{conv}}}$.
MFN Clause (Most Favored Nation): Right to adopt terms of subsequent SAFEs if issued with superior conditions.
Standard Cap: Post-Money (YC)

Pre-Money vs. Post-Money SAFE

Under post-money SAFEs, early investors lock in an exact ownership percentage ($I / \text{Cap}$) prior to the priced round, placing all intermediate dilution risk on common founders.

LEGAL FRAMEWORKS COMPARISON

SAFE vs. Convertible Debt vs. Mútuo Conversível

SAFE (Y Combinator) Not a debt instrument; no maturity date, no interest rate accrual. Reduces legal costs and bankruptcy risk. Equity warrant with conditional triggers (Priced Round, Liquidity Event, Dissolution).
Convertible Note (US Debt) Short-term debt accruing interest ($4\% - 8\%$ p.a.) with a defined maturity date ($12 - 24$ months). Debt must convert or be repaid/renegotiated at maturity.
Mútuo Conversível (Brazil) Most common Brazilian contract. Standard loan structure that provides legal shield against labor and tax liabilities prior to formal equity conversion under Marco Legal das Startups (LC 182/2021).

PITCH DECK ARCHITECTURE

Act I: Hook, Problem & Solution

The first 3 slides determine whether an investor leans in or tunes out:

Slide 01 — Elevator Title: Company name, tagline defining value proposition, institutional affiliation (e.g., UFV/SICon spin-off), and contact info.
Slide 02 — Problem & Urgency: Real-world industrial inefficiency, economic loss, or regulatory barrier. Must be acute and urgent.
Slide 03 — Value Proposition: How the proprietary technology delivers an unfair advantage ($10\times$ faster, $50\%$ lower cost, or zero-carbon output).
PITCH DECK ARCHITECTURE

Act II: Market, Traction & The Ask

The remaining core slides substantiate financial viability, execution velocity, and capital deployment:

Slide 07 — Traction & Milestones: Letters of intent (LOI), paid pilot programs, grant awards (FINEP/FAPEMIG), TRL progression, and patent grants.
Slide 08 — Team & Unfair Advantage: Complementary technical, operations, and business execution capabilities.
Slide 09 — The Ask & Use of Funds: Target round size, SAFE or equity terms, 18-month milestone plan, and runway expansion runway.

Impact Investing, ESG Governance & UN SDGs

Impact Investing Alpha

IMPACT FINANCE TAXONOMY

GIIN, SFDR & ESG Materiality

Impact measurement requires rigorous auditing frameworks beyond standard public relations marketing:

GIIN IRIS+ Standards: Generally accepted impact performance metrics converting engineering outputs into standardized social and environmental KPIs.
SFDR Classification: Article 6 (Standard), Article 8 (Promotes ESG characteristics), and Article 9 (Targeted sustainable investment objective).
Greenwashing Penalties: Misrepresented LCA metrics or unverified carbon offsets trigger regulatory fines and loss of institutional venture capital backing.
SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT GOALS

Engineering Target KPIs

Target metrics for deep tech civil, materials, and infrastructure ventures:

Target 9.4: Upgrade infrastructure and retrofit industries to make them sustainable, with increased resource-use efficiency and greater adoption of clean technologies.
Target 11.5: Significantly reduce economic losses and human risks caused by disasters and structural failures.
Target 12.5 & 13.2: Substantially reduce waste generation through recycling/circular economy and integrate climate change measures into strategic policies.
