Global Policies, TTO Models & Deep-Tech

Multilateral Frameworks

TTOs & University Equity

Technology Transfer Offices manage IP, licensing, and academic spin-offs.

UK policy shift: lowering university spin-out equity to ~16% significantly reduces founder friction and spurs VC capital entry.
