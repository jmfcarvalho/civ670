MODULE 02 — EMPIRICAL FOUNDATIONS

Desk Research & Ethnographic Immersion

Desk Research Protocol

Comprehensive literature, regulatory, and patent indexing to eliminate redundant assumptions before field entry.

  • Peer-reviewed technical literature and benchmark reports.
  • Mandatory norms (ABNT, ASTM, ISO) and environmental codes.
  • Public repositories, trade journals, and market data sources.

Ethnographic Triad

In-situ context absorption to map discrepancies between what industrial actors claim and what actually occurs.

Observation (SEE): Contextual site tracking without interfering; documenting friction points immediately.
Immersion (FEEL): Experiencing user friction directly to capture tacit, unstated constraints.
Interview (LISTEN): Pair interviewing, open-ended inquiry, and active listening without bias.
QUANTITATIVE SIZING (BRANCH Y=1)

TAM / SAM / SOM Filter

Bottom-up mathematical decomposition of aggregate market volume into actionable commercial beachheads.

Methodological Rule: Avoid top-down percentage heuristics; calculate strictly via target customer count $\times$ average annual contract value.

MODULE 03 — QUALITATIVE VALIDATION

Customer Discovery & Root Pain Audit

7-Step Pain Discovery

Structured progression from unverified internal beliefs to quantified operational market demand:

1–2. Assumptions $\rightarrow$ Testable Hypotheses
3–4. Exploratory Interviews $\rightarrow$ Pattern Identification
5–6. In-Depth Interviews $\rightarrow$ Root Pain Isolation
7. Match Critical Pain with Sized TAM/SAM Opportunity

Inquiry Governance

De-biasing user conversations to prevent false positives and flattering speculative answers:

Past & Present Facts: Investigate past expenditures and current habits; never query hypothetical future intentions.
Zero Solution Pitching: Keep the engineering artifact hidden; measure existing alternative workarounds and friction costs.
Pair Interviewing: Conduct in pairs: one investigator drives dialogue while the second logs verbatim quotes.
INTERACTIVE SCRIPT AUDIT (BRANCH Y=1)

Interview Bias Auditor

Stress-test exploratory interview questions against confirmation bias, leading inquiries, and hypothetical distortions.

Methodological Rule: Asking "Would you buy our sustainable cement?" produces useless courtesy bias. Ask "When was the last time you rejected a clinker supplier and why?" instead.

VIRTUAL LAB (BRANCH Y=1)

Customer Interview Script & Bias Auditor

Question Diagnostic Console

Mom Test Rules
Inquiry Guideline: Discard questions querying future intentions, avoid gerunds, and probe exclusively for documented behavior and economic friction.
METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT REPORT SCIENTIFIC GRADE: 95/100
Inquiry Flaw & Epistemic Risk:

None. Probing anchored past episodes forces the respondent to reveal empirical workflow realities rather than polite opinions.

5-Whys Escalation Protocol:

"Why did that failure occur?" $\rightarrow$ "Why wasn't it detected during pre-batching?" $\rightarrow$ Follow root operational chain.

Recommended Reformulation:

Question complies with empirical customer discovery benchmarks. No alteration required.

Verification Target: Real customer pain, actual budgets spent, current workarounds.

MODULE 04 — QUALITATIVE CONVERGENCE

Synthesis, Affinity Clustering & Empathy Maps

Divergence-Convergence

Translating field notes and observation transcripts into structured design opportunities (Design Council, 2005):

1. Story Logging: Extract verbatim statements, observed facts, and emotional reactions.
2. Affinity Clustering: Pivot away from rigid formats; cluster quotes by underlying patterns.
3. Visual Representation: Graph complex stakeholder relations via causal maps.

Evidence Stratification

Isolating subjective interpretations from objective empirical reality across 4 distinct field bins:

Statements Explicit verbal claims made during interviews.
Facts Observable behaviors, recorded telemetry, expenditures.
Feelings Tacit frustrations, anxieties, pride, resistance.
Needs Root functional requirements and latent outcomes.
PERSONA MAPPING (X-RAY VIEW)

Empathy Map Modeling

Consolidate qualitative observations into an empirical stakeholder persona to redefine the core strategic inquiry.

Says & Does: Public operational behavior on the construction site.
Thinks & Feels: Unvoiced economic risk, liability anxiety, aspirations.
Pains: Bottlenecks, delivery delays, compliance fines.
Gains: Productivity boosts, certified safety, margin expansion.

SYNTHESIS FRAMEWORK

Persona & Empathy Specification

The empathy map aggregates direct field evidence into a synthesized operational actor, preventing development based on internal team assumptions:

What They Hear: Influences from general contractors, environmental regulators, suppliers, and competitors.
What They See: Market trends, on-site physical bottlenecks, material waste accumulation, and alternative solutions.
Redefining the Strategic Challenge: Use the synthesized persona profile to reformulate the initial question into a high-leverage value proposition.
MODULE 05 — COMMERCIAL STRATEGY

Beachhead Validation & Problem-Solution Fit

Beachhead Criteria

Selecting a narrow, dominant market segment to concentrate scarce technical and financial resources:

1. Target Well-Funded: Clients have dedicated capital budget to eliminate the specific operational bottleneck.
2. Compelling Value Reason: Failure to solve induces critical operational downtime, severe fines, or structural liability.
3. Strong Word-of-Mouth: Homogeneous references; industry players reference peers directly for standard adoption.

Validation Gates

De-risking technological commercialization through objective empirical proof gates:

Root Pain Evidence: At least 80% of exploratory interviewees actively spend money on inefficient workarounds.
Economic Value Delta: Documented cost, speed, or compliance superiority exceeding incumbent methods by $> 3\times$.
Regulatory Viability: Technology complies with existing industrial codes without requiring complete normative re-write.
EVIDENCE AUDIT

Skin-in-the-Game Ladder

Separate cheap conversational praise from verifiable commercial traction using progressive customer commitment tests:

1 Time Commitment: Client grants access to confidential telemetry data.
2 Reputation Commitment: Client introduces project to decision-making executive.
3 Financial Commitment: Signed Letter of Intent (LOI) or paid field pilot contract.

STRATEGIC FOCUS

Beachhead Selection Rules

A successful beachhead enables an engineering startup to conquer a defensible niche, achieve positive unit economics, and leverage references to conquer adjacent markets:

Disciplined Entrepreneurship Rule: Never pursue multiple market segments simultaneously before dominating the beachhead. Chasing fragmented customers drains technical resources and stalls product refinement.
Whole Product Delivery: Ensure your engineering solution includes everything required to realize the full value proposition (e.g., training, compliance certification, and warranty integration).
