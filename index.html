COURSE INSTRUCTOR & SCIENTIFIC LEAD

Prof. José Maria Franco de Carvalho

Associate Professor | CNPq PQ-C Researcher | Coordinator of PMS & PPGEC — UFV

Academic Profile & Scientific Leadership

Civil Engineer graduated from the Federal University of Viçosa - UFV (2004), Master of Science in Civil Engineering from the Federal University of Ouro Preto - UFOP (2007), and Ph.D. in Civil Engineering from the Federal University of Ouro Preto with a doctoral research internship (sandwich period) at the Federal Institute for Materials Research and Testing in Berlin - BAM, Germany (2019).

He is currently an Associate Professor at the Federal University of Viçosa (UFV), a CNPq Research Productivity Fellow (Level PQ-C), General Coordinator of the Prototyping Center for Materials, Parts, and Systems (PMS/UFV), Coordinator of the Graduate Program in Civil Engineering (PPGEC/UFV), and Co-Leader of the Sustainable & Innovative Construction Research Group (SICon - CNPq/UFV). He actively collaborates with CNPq research groups RECICLOS (UFOP), ATIVE (UFOP), DEC UFV Structures, and TechBIM (UFV).

Primary Technological & Research Focus: Sustainable construction materials, eco-efficient concretes, industrial waste valorization, alkali-activated cementitious matrices, structural repair and rehabilitation of bridge infrastructure, and structural health management.

PEDAGOGICAL & TECHNICAL DISCLOSURE

Statement on the Use of Artificial Intelligence

In alignment with academic transparency, technological innovation, and scientific rigor, the syllabus, slide decks, interactive simulations, and virtual laboratories of CIV 670 were developed through collaborative human-AI co-piloting between the course professor and state-of-the-art Large Language Models.

1. Front-End Architecture & Virtual Lab Synthesis: Generative AI was utilized to architect clean, dependency-light HTML5, TailwindCSS, and vanilla JavaScript simulation engines. This includes the implementation of real-time interactive models (such as LCC discounted cash-flows, LCA carbon accounts, RPN/FMEA matrix calculators, SAFE dilution converters, and dynamic sticky-note canvases).
2. Curricular Structuring & Scaffolding: AI tools assisted in synthesizing international frameworks (NASA TRLs, ISO 14040/44, ISO 31000, Design Thinking, PSS archetypes, and European SFDR directives) into consistent pedagogical matrices calibrated for Full HD and mobile projection.
3. Scientific Curation & Pedagogical Responsibility: All mathematical formulas, legal standards (Brazilian Law 10,973/04 and Law 13,243/16), prompt directives, and engineering concepts were rigorously curated, fact-checked, and validated by the course instructor. The instructor assumes complete intellectual and pedagogical responsibility for all delivered materials.
