FOUNDATIONAL INSTITUTIONAL MODEL

Triple Helix Framework

Formulated by Henry Etzkowitz and Loet Leydesdorff (2000), this framework analyzes non-linear dynamics where Academia, Industry, and Government adopt hybrid capabilities.

Core Dimensions: Evolution of entrepreneurial universities, collaborative R&D, and regulatory seed support.
